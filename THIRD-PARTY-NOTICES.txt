TreeSpitter third-party notices
===============================

TreeSpitter is a C# port of tree-sitter (https://github.com/tree-sitter/tree-sitter), which is licensed under the MIT
License:

- src/TreeSpitter/Dsl/dsl.js reproduces the behavior and the error messages of tree-sitter's
  crates/generate/src/dsl.js (commit 9632b561db2f647e9c0be9e61cf91f10671a6491).
- src/TreeSpitter/Generation ports the parser generator of tree-sitter v0.27.1 (crates/generate: prepare_grammar,
  build_tables, render), and the parser runtime (ParserEngine, ParseStack, SubtreeArena, SyntaxTree) ports
  lib/src/parser.c, stack.c, subtree.c and the S-expression writer of tree-sitter v0.27.1.

The repository vendors grammars and corpora of tree-sitter grammars, under grammars/, used by the tests and the
benchmarks. The TreeSpitter.Languages package contains the parse tables and lexers generated from the grammar.json files
of the JSON, Go, Java and C grammars, under their MIT licenses below. Each directory keeps the license of its project and
a SOURCE.md with its origin. benchmarks/TreeSpitter.Benchmarks/Inputs vendors two source files of tree-sitter (MIT, see
its LICENSE and SOURCE.md) as benchmark inputs; they aren't part of the packages.
